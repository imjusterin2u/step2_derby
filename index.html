THE STEP 2 DERBY

Wilson Reading System · horse-race review

4 LEGS · TIMED RACE

Every correct answer gives your horse a gallop forward and adds 5 seconds to the clock (up to a cap, so a leg always keeps moving). Get 3 in a row and your horse breaks into a Hot Streak — a big burst forward. Each leg is its own mini-race: first horse to the line wins that leg, and whoever wins the most legs takes the Derby. Nothing here gets saved — it's just a fun lap before the End-of-Step check.
